## How to Use This Table

**DIY Cost** is materials only — what you'll spend at the hardware store. It doesn't include tool rental or the value of your time.

**Pro Cost** includes labor and materials and reflects national averages. Urban markets and specialty contractors often run 20–40% higher. Get at least 3 quotes for any job over $500.

**Difficulty ratings:**
- **Beginner** — Basic tool skills, no permits required in most areas. A careful first-timer can tackle these.
- **Intermediate** — Requires some experience or specific technique. Mistakes are fixable but cost time and money.
- **Advanced** — Structural, major electrical, or work requiring permits. DIY is possible but the risk of a costly mistake is real.

## When to Call a Pro

Even skilled DIYers should call a licensed contractor when:
- The repair involves load-bearing walls, the electrical panel, or main plumbing lines
- A permit is required (unpermitted work can create title issues when you sell)
- The repair connects to gas lines
- You find unexpected damage (mold, rot, asbestos) during the initial fix

For everything else, this site has step-by-step guides. Use the [Fix It Right Way guide library](/posts/) to find instructions for the specific repair you're facing.
