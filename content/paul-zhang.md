Paul Zhang is a licensed electrician who decided to start writing about home electrical work after watching too many homeowners attempt unsafe DIY fixes from bad tutorials. He is deeply committed to electrical safety and writes with a clarity that distinguishes what a careful homeowner can do legally and safely from work that genuinely requires a licensed professional. At Fixit Guide, he covers outlets, switches, circuit breakers, lighting, and electrical safety.
